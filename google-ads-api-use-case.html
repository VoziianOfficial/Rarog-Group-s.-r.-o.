Workflow scope

What the API workflow supports

The current use case is organized around authorized reporting, campaign monitoring, and account support rather than unrelated data collection or password handling.

Reporting dashboards

Campaign, ad group, keyword, search term, cost, click, impression, conversion, and conversion value reporting.

Campaign monitoring

Review status, budgets, delivery signals, and performance changes across authorized accounts.

Account management support

Organize authorized client account data for internal review and service delivery.

Budget and performance sync

Synchronize spend, date ranges, conversion metrics, and core campaign indicators.

Optimization review

Help identify areas for improvement based on authorized account performance data.

Client communication

Prepare clearer reporting summaries for clients and internal account reviews.

Data handled

Data handled through the workflow

The workflow is limited to data needed for reporting, monitoring, account review, and authorized Google Ads support.

  • Google Ads customer ID and account identifiers
  • Campaign, ad group, ad, asset, and keyword IDs or names
  • Status, bidding, budget, and delivery settings needed for review
  • Impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, conversion value, CTR, CPC, CPA, and related metrics
  • Search term and performance reports where authorized
  • Date ranges, segments, and reporting filters

The workflow does not request Google Ads login passwords and does not collect payment card data through this website contact form.

Authorization and access

Access is limited to authorized Google Ads account workflows.

Rarog Group uses access controls that match reporting, monitoring, and account management support needs without selling Google Ads account data.

Authorized accounts only

Access is based on authorized Google Ads accounts connected for business reporting and support.

OAuth 2.0 where required

OAuth 2.0 is used for authorization where user or account permission is required.

Revocable access

Users can revoke access through their Google or Google Ads account settings.

Scope limitation

API access is limited to the scope needed for reporting, monitoring, and account management support.

No account data resale

Rarog Group does not sell Google Ads account data obtained through authorized workflows.
