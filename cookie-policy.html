Cookies are small text files stored by a website in a user’s browser. Similar technologies, such as localStorage, may also store small pieces of information in the browser to remember preferences or support website functionality.

This Cookie Policy refers to cookies and similar browser storage technologies together as “cookies” where appropriate.

3. Essential cookies and storage

Essential cookies or browser storage may be used to support core website functionality, security, layout behavior, accessibility features, and user preferences.

In this website, localStorage may be used to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie banner. This helps prevent the banner from reappearing after you make a choice.

4. Preference cookies

Preference cookies or browser storage may remember choices such as cookie consent status or other website preferences. These are used to make the website experience more consistent during future visits.

The current cookie banner stores a choice under a localStorage key so that the website can remember the visitor’s selection.

5. Analytics cookies

If analytics tools are connected to this website, analytics cookies or similar technologies may help understand website performance, traffic sources, page visits, and interaction patterns.

Analytics information can help improve website structure, marketing pages, user experience, and performance measurement. Analytics cookies should not be used to promise guaranteed leads, sales, rankings, revenue, or business outcomes.

6. Advertising and third-party cookies

If advertising, remarketing, tracking, social media, embedded content, or analytics platforms are added to the website, those third-party services may use their own cookies or tracking technologies.

Rarog Group does not control the cookie practices, privacy policies, tracking behavior, or data processing rules of third-party platforms. Users should review the relevant third-party terms and privacy notices where applicable.

7. Cookie banner

The website includes a cookie confirmation banner. The banner stays visible until the user clicks Accept or Decline. After a choice is made, that choice is stored in the browser and the banner should not appear again unless the browser data is cleared or the storage key is reset.

Accepting may allow non-essential cookies or analytics if such tools are connected. Declining should prevent non-essential cookies from being used where technically supported.

8. How to manage cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete stored data, clear localStorage, or set preferences for specific websites.

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect website preferences, saved cookie choices, analytics settings, or certain website functions.

9. Browser storage reset

If you want the cookie banner to appear again, you can clear this website’s browser data in your browser settings. This may remove the saved Accept or Decline choice.

The website may then ask you to choose cookie preferences again on your next visit.

Cookies and browser storage may involve personal data depending on the tools used. For more information about how information may be collected and used, read the Privacy Policy.
